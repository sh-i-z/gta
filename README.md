# GTA — Your Little Escape

Bored? Tired? Avoiding responsibilities?  
Yeah, we've all been there. 👀

Welcome to a tiny 3D world where you can take a break from reality and just vibe.

**What is this?**

A little interactive 3D desktop experience built for fun, curiosity, and those moments when you have absolutely nothing to do.

**Why visit?**
- 🎮 Explore a tiny virtual world
- 🌀 Take a break from the usual scrolling
- 🌙 Just chill. No goals, no pressure.

##  How to Play

A little lost? Don't worry, the world comes with instructions. 👀

- 🕹️ **Move around** and explore your surroundings using letters -> WASD.
- ⎋ **Press `ESC`** to discover the controls and learn more about the commands.
- Experiment, explore, and see what you can find.

**Play here:** [Enter the little world](https://gta-eight-delta.vercel.app/)

Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

*No tutorial pop-ups ruining the vibe. Just you and your curiosity.* 
*Because sometimes, doing nothing is the whole point.* 🖤
